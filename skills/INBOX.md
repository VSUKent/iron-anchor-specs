## Instructions

Use this queue for discoveries that cannot yet be verified, not as a substitute for updating a document when evidence is available. A rough entry beats none. Include concrete evidence and what breaks if the knowledge is ignored. The editor should verify each entry, mark unresolved claims **unverified**, merge verified knowledge into its numbered home with credit, and then remove the processed entry. If evidence conflicts with existing guidance, record both positions and ask the maintainer to decide.
